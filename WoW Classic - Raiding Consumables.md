# Molten Core
1) Raid Start, Prepot Greater Fire Protection Potion and Greater Arcane Protection Potion for Shazzrah.  This will save you from dying in the first danger zone the Caves with Imps.
2) Once the Last Imp is dead, you can Drink another Greater Fire Protection Potion which will Shield Damage from Magmadar.  The Last imp dying to the Time we pull Lucifron is over 2 Minutes so your Potion Cooldown will be back up for Lucifron
3) Lucifron Everyone can drink a Restorative Pot on this boss to completely negate all of his mechanics.  The only exception to this is Warriors who should be drinking Mighty Rage Potions on every boss encounter
4) Magmadar everyones Potion cooldown will be back up.  Raid can use Restorative Pots on this fight to Dispell the Fear if they want to have maximum uptime.  Warriors should still save their Potion CD for Mighty Rage Potion
5) Magmadar dies, you will have time between Magmadar and Clearing Gehennas Trash to Pop another Greater Fire Protection Potion which will shield damage from Gehennas Rain of Fire.  Your potion CD will be back up for Gehennas
6)  Gehennas Melee will use Free Action Potion here on boss pull.  All ranged will use Restorative Potions to remove the Curse.  Only Tanks should be decursed for this fight as its a short fight and Melee should have enough barrier from GFPP
7) Clearing trash towards Garr, you can prepot a Greater Fire Protection Potion to shield damage from Garr minions explosion
8) Garr  Warriors use Mighty Rage pot here, Everyone else in raid can either shield dmg from Minion Explosions if they want to pump damage (Mage AOE) or use nothing at all
9) Moving towards Baron Room, Prepot Greater Fire Protection Potion for the 2-3 Packs we encounter before Baron.
10) Baron  In a perfect world, you were able to get a fresh Greater Fire Protection Potion barrier up AND have your potion Cooldown ready for Baron pull.  Anyone with a mana bar should use Restorative Pot on this boss.  Melee Should have Greater Fire Protection Potion ready in case they get owned.  Warriors should use Mighty Rage potion.
11)  Clearing towards Shazz, can pop Greater Fire Protection Potion to save yourself from the Elemental Trash Packs
12) Shazzrah  Ideally you were able to Prepot a Greater Arcane Protection Potion, and have your Potion Cooldown available during the boss fight to use another if you need it.  Warriors should save CD for Mighty Rage Potion.  Keep in Mind Arcane Protection lasts for 1 hour, so you can pop this at the beginning of the Raid.
13) Continue clearing out of Baron room, can pop one more Greater Fire Protection Potion to save yourself from Elemental Trash Packs
14) Sulfuron Negligible damage from boss, Warriors should use Mighty Rage Potion.  After boss is dead you can prepot Greater Shadow Protection Potion for Domo fight
15) Golemag Negligible damage from boss, Warriors should use Mighty Rage Potion.  After boss is dead you can prepot Greater Fire Protection Potion for Domo fight
16) Domo If you really dont want to die you can pop another Greater Shadow Protection Potion during the fight if your existing one drops.  Warriors should use Mighty Rage Potion.
17)  Prepot Greater Fire Protection Potion on our way to Ragnaros
18) Ragnaros  Save Potion CD for Greater Fire Protection Potion during the encounter, when you need it.  Warriors should use Mighty Rage Potion.

You can easily substitute Regular Fire Protection Potions into this list for the non critical moments, like the Imp Caves or Baron Trash, the Regular Fire Protection potions I can craft for around 60s each, Greater Fires are around 4g-5g
